## Bus 47C 0.12.0 Beta 1

Download **Bus47C-Beta-0.12.0-Windows.zip**, extract the whole folder and run **Bus47C.exe**. Keep the PCK beside the executable. No Godot installation is needed.

This update brings the gameplay closer to the gloomy visual reference, with warmer interior lights against a cold forest.

- The default picture filter now uses muted colour, cooler shadows, fine grain, softer exposure and restrained bloom. It stays active through the morning scenes and can still be disabled in Settings.
- Bus upholstery, paint and ceilings carry procedural wear. Window glass has subtle rain marks and dirty edges, while the windshield stays clearer.
- Damp asphalt catches moving headlight glints. Warm lights cast selected local shadows in the bus, shelter and hallway.
- House lighting is less harsh, and surface grain is less coarse. The journey's gradual dimming and the morning lighting transition are preserved.
- The Escape menu layout and all previously released story, body-dragging, wardrobe and ending mechanics remain intact.

Native captures were reviewed across the cabin, window, forest stop, house, diner, blood, pause menu and morning. Source scene checks and atmosphere-transition checks passed. Road highlights are stylized rather than reflected scene images.

Both routes remain playable. This is a Windows x86_64 beta using OpenGL 3.3, tested on the local AMD Radeon graphics device. Broader hardware and other graphical platforms remain unverified. More lore and polish are planned.

Contains graphic stylized violence, blood and jumpscares. Audio provenance and engine notices are included in the download. Previous releases remain available for rollback.
