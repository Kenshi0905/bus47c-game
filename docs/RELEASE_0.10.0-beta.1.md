# Bus 47C 0.10.0 Beta 1

A presentation and interaction update, with a rebuilt Night Owl Diner and complete closing scenes for both routes. This is still a beta; more lore and polish are planned.

## Download

Download **Bus47C-Beta-0.10.0-Windows.zip**, extract it completely and run **Bus47C.exe** with **Bus47C.pck** beside it. Choose **New Journey** to see the full current sequence. No Godot installation is required.

Windows x86_64, OpenGL 3.3. Headphones recommended. The release includes **SHA256SUMS.txt**, build information and third-party notices. Other graphical platforms and broader hardware support remain unverified.

## Changes

- Red vinyl diner booths, cream tables, counter stools, window blinds and warmer overhead lighting, with an open central aisle.
- The selected distressed BUS 47C logo and matching Share Tech Mono typography throughout the interface. A simpler README with one game image.
- Irregular, tapered blood strokes on the hazard sign.
- Generic E inspection on town bodies, with shocked reactions and personal clues instead of identity labels.
- Limited movement and free look inside the wardrobe. Hold right mouse to peek. A countdown shows the time until daylight.
- Timed progress displays for engine repair, burial and coffee preparation. Pausing and notes preserve progress and movement locks.
- An illustrated, column-based newspaper and matching physical prop. Both final documents now lead into first-person closing scenes, audio and credits before results.
- Cleaner journal completion marks, separate abandoned-route text and clean audio shutdown.

## Validation

The pinned Godot import, route tests and full scene suite passed on Windows. Checks cover both routes, physical shop traversal, passengers, deer timing, pursuit, repairs, burial, checkpoints and endings. Additional native checks exercise closet mouse look, movement, peeking and pause recovery. The changed scenes and menus were reviewed in native rendered captures.

This beta includes four shop interiors and House 17. Other houses are scenery. The mirror uses a staged portrait and defense is a close-range interaction. Contains blood, disturbing imagery and jumpscares.
