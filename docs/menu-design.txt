game/assets/ui/fonts/ShareTechMono-Regular.ttf
game/assets/ui/fonts/ShareTechMono-OFL.txt

SIL Open Font License. The regular face serves readable body text and world

EDITABLE UI
game/scripts/main.gd: native controls, title camera, document layout.
game/assets/ui/fonts/menu_font.tres: menu type and tracking.
game/assets/ui/selection-brush.svg: focused menu highlight.
game/assets/ui/menu_atmosphere.gdshader: title grain and vignette.
game/scripts/story_presentation.gd: timed action UI and closing scenes.
game/scripts/shift_journal.gd: completed, active and abandoned tasks.
game/scripts/newspaper.gd: newspaper masthead, columns, photographs and copy.
game/assets/ui/newsprint.gdshader: monochrome printed-photo treatment.
tools/press_photos.gd: render the press photographs from editable game models.

The diner uses procedural booths, counters, stools, blinds and lights in
game/scripts/town_district.gd. Its vinyl and tile treatments remain editable
in game/assets/ui/aged_surface.gdshader. No reference photograph is shipped.
