# Bus 47C 0.13.0 Beta 1

This beta refines the bus journey, House 17 and both late-game routes. More lore and final polish are still in development.

## Changes

- Getting off at the wrong stop triggers a choking encounter with a working retry.
- Everyone leaves the bus by St Bride's, before the final stop.
- New editable stag model with antlers, long ears, a proper muzzle, articulated legs and cloven hooves.
- Clearer firewatcher introduction and a quiet hunger line after leaving the broken bus.
- Rebuilt town road joins and pavements, corrected House 17 floors and removed the placeholder hall chair.
- Forestry Way has trail landmarks, a forgotten checkpoint and a path continuing into the fog beyond the collapse.
- Approaching the mirror triggers gradual deterioration of the reflection's face.
- The first-person collapse uses a jointed body that falls, settles and changes from the original uniform into the suited man.
- Wider wardrobe peeking reveals roaming figures and hell hounds. Holding it open too long attracts attention.
- Bodies are arranged in crossed, posed layers instead of a flat cluster.
- Repair, burial and coffee progress use compact pixel bars without boxed panels.
- Visible coffee preparation includes the tin, spoon, hotplate, kettle, cup, pouring, stirring and steam.

The existing gloomy filter and pause-menu layout are retained. The two new spoken lines use temporary synthetic performances that can be replaced by actor recordings.

## Play

Download **Bus47C-Beta-0.13.0-Windows.zip** below, extract the whole archive and run **Bus47C.exe**. Keep the PCK beside the executable. Choose **New Journey** to see the revised beginning; existing checkpoints can still resume later scenes.

Windows x86_64 with OpenGL 3.3. Contains graphic stylized violence, blood and jumpscares. Other graphical platforms remain unverified.

## Validation

The scene suite covers both routes and endings, all four forbidden bus exits, checkpoint retries, the final passenger evacuation, timed peeking, automatic mirror deterioration, the physical fall and staged coffee preparation. Native scene captures were reviewed for roads, house floors, the stag, body mound, wardrobe, mirror, coffee and transformation.
