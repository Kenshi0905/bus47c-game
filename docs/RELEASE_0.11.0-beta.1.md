## Bus 47C 0.11.0 Beta 1

Download **Bus47C-Beta-0.11.0-Windows.zip**, extract the whole folder and run **Bus47C.exe**. Keep the PCK beside the executable. No Godot installation is needed.

This beta fixes body dragging and the Forestry Way collapse, and adds the wardrobe and forest consequences.

- Dragged bodies stay grounded, follow your footsteps and collide with walls and furniture. Looking around no longer swings the corpse. Press E to release it.
- The house attacker on the bus route wears a dark suit and glowing red tie, including after falling.
- The wardrobe countdown now ends with a manual exit. Wait for daylight and birdsong, then press E. Leaving early plays an eleven-second first-person attack with hounds, growing red vision and a visible Try Again screen.
- On the footpath route, leaving town before completing the mirror scene triggers a separate forest encounter. The town and shops remain safe.
- The Forestry Way reveal uses a full-size body resting on the path. The opening establishes the protagonist as a firewatcher.
- Blood pools, footprints and dripping signs remain visibly red under night lighting.
- Includes the atmosphere pass with progressively colder cabin lighting, deeper mist and quieter gaps in the drone.

Both routes, shops, endings and checkpoint retries remain playable. More lore and polish are still planned. This is a Windows x86_64 beta using OpenGL 3.3; wider hardware and graphical platform support remain unverified.

Contains graphic stylized violence, blood and jumpscares. Audio provenance and engine notices are included in the download.

Previous releases remain available for rollback. Report bugs with the version, route and steps to reproduce them.
